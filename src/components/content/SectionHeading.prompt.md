**SectionHeading** — opens nearly every section: eyebrow kicker, Playfair title, gold divider, optional subtitle.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="The Banquet Lawns"
  title="Celebrations Beneath the Open Sky"
  subtitle="Sprawling lawns for up to 1000 guests, framed by mature trees and warm festoon light."
/>
<SectionHeading align="left" tone="light" eyebrow="Guest House" title="Four Boutique Suites" />
```

Use `tone="light"` on forest backgrounds, `align="left"` inside split layouts.
