**QuoteBlock** — centered guest testimonial: gold quote mark, Playfair italic quote, name + event attribution.

```jsx
<QuoteBlock
  quote="They turned our wedding into pure magic — the lawns at dusk were unforgettable."
  author="Aarti & Rohan"
  role="Wedding · November 2025"
/>
```

Use `tone="light"` over a forest band.
