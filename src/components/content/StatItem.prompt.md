**StatItem** — a headline figure (gold Playfair) over an uppercase label. Use in a row on a forest band to convey scale.

```jsx
<StatItem value="1000+" label="Guest Capacity" tone="light" />
<StatItem value="18" label="Acres of Lawns" tone="light" />
<StatItem value="4" label="Boutique Suites" tone="light" />
```
